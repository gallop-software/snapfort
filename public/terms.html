SnapFort.io

Terms, refunds and privacy · SnapFort is a game by Web Plant Media LLC.

The game

SnapFort is a free building game played in the web browser, in shared online worlds. The Meadow world is free for everyone.

The Full Game ($7, one time)

  • Lets you build in every paid world (the Moon and Mars so far), including paid worlds added later. Anyone may visit a paid world without it.
  • It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Payment is by card through Stripe.
  • After paying you get a SnapFort key (like SNAP-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX), by email too. The device you bought on is unlocked and remembers it; to unlock another, open the email there and tap "Unlock SnapFort on this device" (or type the key in Settings).
  • A key has seats, $7 each: one seat lets one person build at a time, on any of your devices. Buy several seats together, or add seats later in Settings, and that many people can build at the same time with the one key (a family, say), each on their own device.
  • When every seat is in use, one more device visits until a seat is free. It can take a seat with "Use it here": whoever has played longest goes back to visiting.
  • If your key is shared or leaked, use "Replace my key" in Settings: we email you a link, and once you tap it the old key stops working and you get a new one.
  • What you build lives in a shared world that other players can change and knock down, as part of the game. Worlds may be reset or changed while the game develops.

Refunds

Not happy? Email us within 14 days of buying and we will refund you in full, no questions asked. After that, write to us anyway and we will do our best.

Support and lost keys

Lost your key? In Settings choose "Email me a link" and enter the address you paid with. Anything else: email dev@webplant.media.

Privacy

  • You don't need an account to play. Your builder's name and colour are saved only in your browser, with a random id for this browser, so that blocks and bans last between visits. Other players see only a scrambled form of that id, never the id itself.
  • When you buy the Full Game, Stripe handles your card details; we never see them. We keep the email address you paid with, only to send you your key, links to unlock your devices, and help with your purchase.
  • Chat messages are passed to other players and not stored. The exception is a report: when a player reports someone, we keep the report and the last few things the reported player said, so we can look into it. What you build is saved as part of the shared world.
  • We don't sell your information or show ads.

Playing fairly

Be kind in chat. Messages and names are filtered for swear words, links and personal details. If someone bothers you, open Settings while playing and choose Players · Block or report: blocking hides their chat on your device, and reporting tells us. We look at every report. Harassment, cheating, spamming or trying to break the game can get you removed.
