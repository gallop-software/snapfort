Terms, refunds and privacy · SnapFort is a game by Web Plant Media LLC.

The game

SnapFort is a free building game played in the web browser, in shared online worlds. The Meadow world is free for everyone.

The Full Game ($7, one time)

  • Lets you build in every paid world, starting with the Moon, including paid worlds added later. Anyone may visit a paid world without it.
  • It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Payment is by card through Stripe.
  • After paying you get a SnapFort key (like SNAP-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX). It is saved in your browser, and you can type it into Settings on your other devices to unlock them too. Please keep it somewhere safe.
  • What you build lives in a shared world that other players can change and knock down, as part of the game. Worlds may be reset or changed while the game develops.

Refunds

Not happy? Email us within 14 days of buying and we will refund you in full, no questions asked. After that, write to us anyway and we will do our best.

Support and lost keys

Email dev@webplant.media. If you lost your key, tell us the email address you paid with and we will send it to you.

Privacy

  • You don't need an account to play. Your builder's name and colour are saved only in your browser.
  • When you buy the Full Game, Stripe handles your card details; we never see them. We keep the email address you paid with, only so we can help you find a lost key.
  • Chat messages are passed to other players and never stored. What you build is saved as part of the shared world.
  • We don't sell your information or show ads.

Playing fairly

Be kind in chat. Messages are filtered for swear words, links and personal details. Cheating, spamming or trying to break the game can get you removed.
